# Portfolio upgrade outcomes

## Faster cinematic first impression
The portfolio keeps its cinematic identity while showing meaningful content sooner: the entrance loader is shorter, does not trap visitors for an unnecessary wait, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

## More polished editorial hierarchy
The home page makes the positioning, chapter structure, work, leadership, and contact actions easier to scan while retaining the existing warm-black, signal-orange visual language and interactive personality.

## Responsive on every screen
Navigation, hero composition, project rows, timeline content, footer, and contact tools remain usable at mobile, tablet, laptop, and wide desktop widths without relying on hover-only behavior.

## Accessible interaction
Interactive controls have semantic labels and keyboard-friendly states; custom cursor and motion are progressive enhancements and do not interfere with touch or reduced-motion users.

## SEO and deployment alignment
Canonical, Open Graph, Twitter, robots, sitemap, and route declarations consistently describe the current public portfolio origin and routes, with meaningful initial HTML content preserved for crawlers.

## Validation evidence
The project passes ESLint and the production build, and the deployed-style raw HTTP response exposes the intended metadata, sitemap, robots file, and route manifest.
